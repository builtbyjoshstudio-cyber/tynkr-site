03

How your data lives.

Open formats

Hearth keeps your purchase in eight plain JSON files — scenarios, savings, cash-to-close, properties, roadmap, document checklist — plus a folder of the property photos you add. Nothing about your home purchase ever leaves your machine.

No proprietary database. No export fee. No account standing between you and your own files. If Hearth disappeared tomorrow, this folder still works. Turn on optional encryption and the files are locked with your password; leave it off and they open in any text editor.

Fair questions.

FAQ
Is Hearth a mortgage lender or broker?

No. Hearth is planning software. It doesn’t sell loans, take referral fees, or send your information to lenders — it has no way to, since nothing leaves your machine.

Where do my documents live?

Wherever you already keep them. Hearth doesn’t store your documents — it tracks a checklist of the eleven a purchase usually needs, with a status and a note for each. Your PDFs stay in your own folders.

Are the mortgage numbers accurate?

The math is standard amortization plus the taxes, insurance, PMI, and fee figures you enter. It’s a planning estimate — your lender’s Loan Estimate is the binding document.

Is there a subscription?

No. Hearth is a one-time purchase when it launches. Bug fixes are always free; major version upgrades are a separate purchase. No account, no recurring billing.

What happens to my data after I close?

It stays yours. Your JSON files and property photos stay in your data folder as a record of the purchase — and you can export a .zip backup or CSVs any time.
